# Notice

A Minecraft Bedrock horror addon.

> The longer you're alone in the dark, the closer something is allowed to stand behind you.

There is nothing in this pack that will chase you, hit you, or make a noise when
it arrives. There is a figure that stands where you are not looking, and a
hidden number that decides how close it is permitted to get.

---

## Install

**Built pack:** run `./build.sh` and open `dist/notice.mcaddon`. Bedrock imports
both packs; enable them on a world under *Behaviour Packs* and *Resource Packs*.

**Requirements:** Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.20 or later, with the **Beta APIs**
experiment enabled on the world. The pack is script-driven and will do nothing
at all without it.

To iterate on a local install, symlink `packs/notice_BP` and `packs/notice_RP`
into `development_behavior_packs/` and `development_resource_packs/` — the
folders are laid out as shipping packs, so no build step is needed.

---

## What actually happens

You spawn. Nothing happens, because nothing has noticed you yet.

Then you go underground, alone, and stop placing torches. Somewhere around
three minutes in, the fog tightens by a few blocks and a figure is standing at
the edge of your torchlight, thirty metres out, half behind a stalagmite. It
does not move. It does not react. If you walk toward it and look away for half
a second, it is gone.

If you keep looking at it, it learns. The next time it appears it will be
further away and better hidden — and you will have fed the number that decides
how close it is allowed to come next time.

Around twenty minutes of solitary dark, that permitted distance has closed from
thirty-four metres to about six, and it no longer needs to be in front of you.

Dying does not reset this. Nothing resets this completely.

## Counterplay

| | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Daylight | −0.22/s | Useless while you're mining |
| Torches and lanterns | −0.05/s | Slows you down; costs coal |
| Another player within 24 m | −0.12/s | Someone has to come with you |
| A villager within 16 m | −0.07/s | Only works where people live |
| The Kindler | −1.6/s | Guts out if you approach too fast |
| Tallow candle | Accrual ×0.35 for 6 min | The only thing that lowers the floor |

Craft a tallow candle from string over two honeycomb. It is the one item in the
pack, and the only way to genuinely undo a bad night rather than wait one out.

---

## Diagnostics

`/scriptevent nx:state` prints your current notice, the floor beneath it, your
tier, the Watcher's stealth tier, and its permitted radius in metres.

`/scriptevent nx:set 70` forces notice to a value, for testing the upper bands
without spending twenty minutes in a hole.

## Development

```
./build.sh          # regenerate textures, validate, simulate, package
python3 tools/validate.py     # structural checks only
node --import ./tools/harness/hook.mjs tools/harness/sim.mjs   # behaviour only
```

`tools/harness/` runs the pack's real scripts against a simulated voxel world
with a working raycaster, and asserts the one thing the design cannot survive
being wrong about: across ~350 relocations per run, with two players looking in
different directions, the Watcher is never seen moving.

Textures are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` — there is no image library
in the toolchain, so the PNG encoder is hand-rolled on zlib. The pack ships no
audio of its own; every sound is a vanilla Bedrock event played at an unusual
pitch.

See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the full specification.

## Layout

```
packs/notice_BP/          behaviour pack
  scripts/
    config.js             every tunable in the pack
    notice.js             the number
    watcher.js            the observer loop
    support.js            the Hollow and the Kindler
    atmosphere.js         fog, ambient audio, heartbeat
    structures.js         aperture generation and trespass
    counterplay.js        the candle, and diagnostics
packs/notice_RP/          resource pack: geometry, textures, fog, lang
tools/                    texture generation, validation, simulation, packaging
```
